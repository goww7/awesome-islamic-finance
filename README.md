## Standards & Regulatory Bodies

- [AAOIFI](https://aaoifi.com/) - The Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions, issuer of the most widely adopted Shariah standards.
- [IFSB](https://www.ifsb.org/) - Islamic Financial Services Board, prudential standards for Islamic banking, capital markets and takaful.
- [IIFM](https://www.iifm.net/) - International Islamic Financial Market, standardized documentation for Islamic capital and money markets.
- [Securities Commission Malaysia](https://www.sc.com.my/) - Regulator publishing the official list of Shariah-compliant securities on Bursa Malaysia twice a year.

## Screening & Compliance Data

- [Halal Terminal](https://api.halalterminal.com) * - Stock and ETF screening across AAOIFI, DJIM, FTSE, MSCI and S&P methodologies, with purification rates, via web or API. Free tier.
- [Islamicly](https://www.islamicly.com/) - Shariah stock screening app covering global exchanges, with scholar-reviewed verdicts.
- [Musaffa](https://musaffa.com/) - Halal stock and ETF screening platform with purification calculations and portfolio tracking.
- [Zoya](https://zoya.finance/) - Shariah stock screening and portfolio monitoring app, AAOIFI-based, popular in North America.

## Investing Apps & Platforms

- [Finispia](https://finispia.com/) - Shariah screening and halal portfolio tools for retail investors.
- [Hyssa](https://hyssa.com/) - Halal investment app combining screening, alerts and purification tools.
- [Sarwa](https://www.sarwa.co/) - MENA robo-advisor and trading platform offering halal portfolio options.
- [Tabadulat](https://tabadulat.com/) - Shariah-compliant brokerage platform with a built-in halal screener.
- [Wahed](https://www.wahed.com/) - Global halal robo-advisor and investment platform, regulated in multiple jurisdictions.

## Halal ETFs & Funds

- [Amana Mutual Funds](https://www.saturna.com/amana) - The longest-running family of US Islamic mutual funds, managed by Saturna Capital.
- [SP Funds](https://www.sp-funds.com/) - US-listed Shariah-compliant ETFs including SPUS (S&P 500 based) and SPSK (sukuk).

## Zakat & Purification

- [Islamic Relief Zakat Calculator](https://islamic-relief.org/zakat-calculator/) - Zakat calculator from one of the largest Muslim charities.
- [LaunchGood](https://www.launchgood.com/) - Muslim crowdfunding platform widely used for zakat and sadaqah distribution.
- [National Zakat Foundation](https://nzf.org.uk/) - UK institution collecting and distributing zakat locally, with calculation guidance.
- [Zakat Foundation of America](https://www.zakat.org/) - Zakat education and distribution, with practical guides on zakat for stocks and retirement accounts.

## Sukuk & Fixed Income

- [IIFM Sukuk Report](https://www.iifm.net/sukuk-reports/) - Annual reference report on the global sukuk market.
- [Sukuk screening research](https://www.halalterminal.com/research/sukuk-screening) * - Structural analysis of sukuk under AAOIFI Standard 62, with a screenable registry.
- [S&P Dow Jones Sukuk Index](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/fixed-income/dow-jones-sukuk-index/) - Benchmark index methodology for investment-grade sukuk.

## Education & Research

- [AIMS Islamic Finance Academy](https://aims.education/) - Accredited online certifications and degrees in Islamic finance.
- [Ethica Institute](https://www.ethica.institute/) - Islamic finance certification body with AAOIFI-aligned training.
- [INCEIF University](https://www.inceif.edu.my/) - Malaysia's global university of Islamic finance.
- [IsDB Institute](https://isdbinstitute.org/) - Research arm of the Islamic Development Bank, publications on Islamic economics and finance.
- [Islamic Finance Guru](https://www.islamicfinanceguru.com/) - Practical halal money content: investing, pensions, mortgages and startup investing.
- [Islamic Inheritance Calculator](https://islamic-inheritancecalculator.com/) - Free Faraid inheritance calculator with Hanafi, Ahl-e-Hadith and Shia modes, plus 20 illustrated guides on Islamic inheritance rules.
- [Practical Islamic Finance](https://practicalislamicfinance.com/) - Independent halal investing research and portfolio commentary.

## Academic Library Guides
